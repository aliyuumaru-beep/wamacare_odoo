## Product Definition

> **WamaCare is a Community Impact Management Platform that enables transparent delivery, safeguarding, measurement and sustainability of programmes serving women and girls.**

WamaCare is built on Odoo 17 Community Edition, configured for Nigerian CBOs and NGOs. It is designed as a reusable template deployable across multiple organisations with minimal adaptation.

---

## Mission

To equip Community-Based Organisations with a structured, transparent, and accountable digital platform that enables them to deliver measurable impact to women and girls — while satisfying donor accountability requirements and progressively reducing dependency on external funding.

---

## Vision

A world where every CBO serving women and girls operates with the same governance discipline, financial transparency, and data integrity as a professionally managed institution — regardless of size, location, or funding level.

WamaCare transforms CBOs from:

| From | To |
|------|----|
| Manual, paper-based processes | Digital, auditable workflows |
| Grant-dependent implementers | Structured, transparent organisations |
| Impact claimed, not measured | Impact measured, reported, and verified |
| Donor-facing only | Beneficiary-centred with stakeholder accountability |
| Single deployment | Scalable, reusable template |

## Primary Beneficiaries

- Women accessing maternal health services
- Girls and women at risk of gender-based violence
- Women and girls accessing economic empowerment and skills training
- Community members receiving health capacity-building

## Expected Outcomes

**Operational Outcomes:**
- 100% of programme expenditure traceable to analytic account (programme/donor)
- All LPOs approved through documented workflow before commitment
- All staff records maintained with skills, training, and performance history
- Equipment and assets tracked with maintenance logs

**Impact Outcomes:**
- Cost-per-beneficiary computable per programme per quarter
- Beneficiary count verifiable per programme, per area, per period
- Safeguarding incidents tracked, escalated, and resolved on record
- Donor reports generated from system data — no manual compilation

**Sustainability Outcomes:**
- CBO transitions from Stage 1 (informal) → Stage 2 (digitised) → Stage 3 (enterprise hub)
- Reduced audit preparation time by 70%+
- First-time donor reporting done from system within 60 days of deployment

## Value Proposition

### For CBOs
- A complete, affordable operational platform built for their context (Nigerian, NGO, community-focused)
- Donor-grade accountability without donor-grade cost
- A template that grows with the organisation from basic operations to ecosystem integration

### For Donors
- Real-time financial transparency: budget vs actual per grant, per programme
- Audit trail: every purchase, approval, and payment on record
- Impact data: beneficiary counts, activities completed, outcomes tracked
- Confidence that funds are managed with institutional discipline

### For Beneficiaries
- Safer services: safeguarding workflows protect against harm
- More consistent delivery: programmes are planned, tracked, and evaluated
- Dignified data handling: anonymised records, privacy-by-design

### For the Template Market
- First Odoo 17 NGO/CBO template optimised for Nigerian and African community organisations
- Deployable in days, not months
- Open-source base (Odoo Community) — no recurring licence fees

## What WamaCare Is NOT

| Not this | Why |
|---------|-----|
| A clinical/hospital system | WamaCare delivers health programmes, not clinical care |
| An HMO or insurance system | Not designed for claims processing |
| A generic charity donation platform | Purpose-built for operational CBO management |
| A standalone mobile app | Web-based ERP — mobile access via browser |
| A data collection tool | Operational ERP — not a survey tool (though integration is planned) |
